Black Friday is Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November — but you do not have to wait until Friday. The streaming deals are already live, and most of them end on 30 November. The Deals That Are Actually Live

Paramount+ — $2.99 a month for 2 months

Either plan — Essential or Premium — for $2.99 a month for two months, for new and returning subscribers. After that it goes back to the regular price: $8.99 a month for Essential or $13.99 a month for Premium. This is the best streaming price of the season so far, and it is confirmed by every deal tracker we checked.

Peacock Select — $2.99 a month for 3 months (code PEA4J3K58GUOWW)

Three months of Peacock Select for $2.99 a month with the code PEA4J3K58GUOWW. Read the small print: Select is the cheapest tier, normally $8.99 a month, and it does not include live sports or Peacock originals. Full Peacock Premium costs $12.99 a month and Premium Plus (no ads) is $19.99 a month — neither is part of this deal.

Apple TV — $6.99 a month for 6 months

Apple TV for $6.99 a month for six months, available direct from Apple and through Prime Video and Roku. Six months is the longest cheap window of any deal on this page.

Frndly TV — 99 cents a month for 3 months (ends 1 December)

Frndly TV for 99 cents a month for three months, then $12.99 a month. It is a smaller service — 50+ channels including Hallmark and The Weather Channel — but at 99 cents it is the cheapest live-TV deal of Black Friday. This one ends 1 December, a day after Cyber Monday.

YouTube TV — $15 off a month for 5 months (ends Thanksgiving)

New YouTube TV members save $15 a month for the first five months — about $67.99 a month instead of $82.99 for the base plan. This one ends first: deal pages say Thanksgiving, Thursday 26 November, so grab it before then. (A couple of trackers list a later end date, but Thanksgiving is the date most of them agree on — do not risk waiting.)

Prime Video Channels — up to 75% off through 30 November

Amazon's Prime Video Channels are up to 75% off through 30 November, for the first two months: MGM+ for $1.99 a month, STARZ for $2.99 a month and AMC+ for $2.75 a month. You need Prime Video to add a channel, but the channels themselves are the deal.

Disney+ and Hulu bundle — $12.99 a month with ads

The Disney+ and Hulu bundle is $12.99 a month with ads or $21.99 a month without ads. It is not a Black Friday special — it is the regular bundle price — but it is still the cheapest way to get both services together, and it is worth knowing before you buy either one alone.

The $1.99 Peacock trap: Peacock's famous Black Friday deal — a full year for $19.99, or $1.99 a month for six months — ran in 2024. In 2025 there was no standalone Peacock price cut at all. For 2026, Peacock has not announced a $1.99 deal, and the only live offer is the Select plan at $2.99 a month with the code above. Any page still promising $1.99 for 2026 is showing you 2024's deal.

Wait — These Have Not Announced a Deal Yet

HBO Max and Hulu (standalone) had still not announced a Black Friday 2026 deal when deal trackers last updated on 22 November. Both ran famous deals in past years — HBO Max at $2.99 a month, Hulu at 99 cents a month for a year — so it is worth checking back before 27 November. Do not pay full price for either one until Black Friday itself.

Netflix never discounts, and 2026 is no exception. Its US prices are $8.99 a month with ads, $19.99 for Standard and $26.99 for Premium, unchanged since the last increase on 26 March 2026. If you are waiting for a Netflix Black Friday deal, stop waiting — there is not one.

How to Get the Deals (Without Losing One)

  1. Check if you qualify: almost every deal above is for new and returning subscribers only. If you already pay for a service, cancel first and wait for the subscription to end — then sign up again with the deal.
  2. Watch the end dates: most deals end 30 November. YouTube TV ends first (Thanksgiving, 26 November); Frndly TV runs to 1 December.
  3. Set a renewal reminder: every deal above rolls onto the full monthly price when the cheap months run out. Put the renewal date in your calendar the day you sign up.
  4. Check the plan, not just the price: Peacock Select at $2.99 is cheap because it leaves out sports and originals. Paramount+ at $2.99 covers both plans. The cheapest price is not always the best deal for what you watch.
