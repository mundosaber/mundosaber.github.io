Marvel Rivals 2nd Anniversary: 2,500 Units and Free Deadpool Skin — When Each Reward Actually Unlocks

By Mark Smith  ·  23 November 2026  ·  Gaming

The headline is everywhere: "2,500 free Units + a free Deadpool skin." It is wrong in two ways. First, you cannot claim the Deadpool skin yet — it unlocks through a separate event that does not open until 26 November. Second, the 2,500 Units are a season total split across three dates, not a day-one grant. This page gives you the correction first, then the full calendar — so you know exactly what you can claim today, what opens on 26 November, and what arrives on 6 December.

The Unlock Calendar

  • 19 Nov — Anniversary Check-In opens: 9-day login event, 1,900 Units total (400 regular Units + 1,500 Limited-Time Units). Claim from Galacta Bot in the Galactic Center, Times Square. Days 1–5 are open now; Days 6–9 unlock 26 Nov.
  • 23 Nov (today) — Twitch Drops begin: IGNITE Grand Finals drops start today, with further waves on 27, 28 and 29 Nov, and the final wave open until 27 Dec. Rewards include the IGNITE Black Widow (2026) costume, Units and an exclusive dynamic spray.
  • 26 Nov — Ghostworks Arcade opens: play the arcade activities to earn up to 400 Units, plus other anniversary-themed rewards.
  • 26 Nov — Van Dyne: Idea Matrix opens: the free Legendary Deadpool costume is earned by completing event activities here. It is NOT a login reward, and the mission requirements have not been revealed yet.
  • 6 Dec — Year in Review + Countdown: 200 more Units arrive with the Year in Review and Countdown events.
  • 11 Dec — expiry day: the 1,500 Limited-Time Units expire at 09:00 UTC. Spend them before then, or they are gone.

What You Can Claim Today (23 November)

Log in and visit Galacta Bot in Times Square. Days 1–5 of the check-in are open now: Day 1 gives 200 permanent Units, and Days 2–5 give 200 Limited-Time Units each — 1,000 Units in total so far. The remaining 900 Units (Days 6–9: 200 permanent + 700 Limited-Time) stay locked until 26 November. Twitch Drops for the IGNITE Grand Finals also start today — tune in to the official livestreams and watch for the required time.

Heads up — the expiry trap: 1,500 of the 1,900 login Units are Limited-Time Units, and they expire on 11 December at 09:00 UTC. Only 400 of the login Units are permanent. If you let the Limited-Time Units sit unspent past 11 December, they disappear.

The Deadpool Skin — What We Actually Know

The free Legendary Deadpool costume comes from the Van Dyne: Idea Matrix event, which opens on 26 November. You earn it by completing event activities — it is not a login reward, and finishing all nine login days will not unlock it on its own. The exact mission requirements and the costume's final name have not been published yet; they are expected when the Week 2 phase begins on 26 November. Until then, any site listing "how to unlock the Deadpool skin" step by step is guessing.

Season 11: Doomsday Is Already Live

The anniversary sits on top of Season 11: Doomsday, which launched on 13 November 2026 with new Strategist Nightcrawler. Dracula joins the roster later in the season. The anniversary events run alongside the season until 11 December.

How to Get Every Unit

  1. Log in every day until 11 Dec and claim from Galacta Bot in Times Square — 1,900 Units across the 9-day check-in (Days 6–9 from 26 Nov).
  2. From 26 Nov, play Ghostworks Arcade — up to 400 Units for taking part in its activities.
  3. On 6 Dec, open Year in Review and the Countdown event — 200 more Units.
  4. Spend the Limited-Time Units before 11 Dec, 09:00 UTC — 1,500 of them expire then.
  5. Watch the IGNITE Grand Finals (from 23 Nov) for Twitch Drops, including the Black Widow costume.

Quick Answers

Can I claim the free Deadpool skin today?
No. The Van Dyne: Idea Matrix event opens on 26 November, and the skin is earned through its activities — not by logging in.

Are the 2,500 Units a login bonus?
No. They are a season total split across three dates: 1,900 from the login check-in, 400 from Ghostworks Arcade (26 Nov), and 200 on 6 Dec.

Do the Limited-Time Units expire?
Yes — the 1,500 Limited-Time Units expire on 11 December at 09:00 UTC. The 400 regular Units do not.

What are the Twitch Drops?
Watch the Marvel Rivals IGNITE Grand Finals livestreams from 23 November and you earn drops for watch time — including the IGNITE Black Widow (2026) costume, Units and a dynamic spray.
