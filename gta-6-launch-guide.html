GTA 6 Launch Week: 7 Things to Know Before Your First Session

By Jeffrey Smith  ·  23 November 2026  ·  Gaming

Grand Theft Auto VI is out. After years of waiting and two delays, Rockstar's biggest game launched on Thursday, 19 November 2026 — and launch week is exactly when the avoidable mistakes happen: the wrong edition, no storage space, a boxed copy that still needs a full download. Here are 7 things to know before your first session. Every fact below is verified against Rockstar, the PlayStation and Xbox stores, or named reporting — nothing here is a guess, a leak, or a first impression.

ADVERTISEMENT

1. It launched 19 November — on consoles only

GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. The game unlocked at midnight local time on 19 November. There is no PC version at launch, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. It is also not on PS4 or Xbox One.

2. Clear up to 212GB — and leave extra room

Rockstar lists the pre-load at up to 212GB, depending on your device. The Xbox Store listing goes higher, at a maximum install size of about 225GB on Xbox. On a base Xbox Series S (512GB model, around 364GB usable), the game alone takes more than half the drive. A launch-era PS5 (825GB model, roughly 667GB usable) is hit hard too.

Expect a day-one update on top — nobody has confirmed its size, so leave a gap on your drive rather than filling it to the last gigabyte. Delete something before you start the download, not halfway through it.

3. The price: $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate

The Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99. The Ultimate Edition is digital only, and adds premium vehicles, weapons and apparel across the story. The $20 difference buys the Ultimate Edition upgrade content — the Standard Edition is the full game.

4. Pre-loading opened 10 November — and the boxed copy is a code, not a disc

Digital pre-orders could pre-load from 10 November (PS5 at midnight local time, Xbox at midnight GMT). Boxed code-in-box copies reached stores from 12 November — but the physical version contains a download code rather than a game disc, so it still downloads the whole game. Buying the box does not save you the download.

5. The pre-order record — an estimate, not a confirmed total

Research firm Sensor Tower estimated 7.71 million digital pre-orders by 3 November 2026, worth an estimated $751 million, with PlayStation accounting for about 74% of units. That is close to Cyberpunk 2077's 8 million pre-order record. But label it correctly: it is an estimate. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed any pre-order total. What is confirmed is that Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on 9 November, in a Reuters report, that pre-orders were far ahead of the company's initial expectations.

6. What is actually confirmed about the game

Only the verified basics: the game brings players back to Vice City, with a new story and two central characters, Jason and Lucia. Rockstar says GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on all current consoles, including the PS5 Pro. That is the confirmed picture. This page does not invent gameplay details, map details, or "first impressions" — if a guide tells you exactly how the map plays before the game has been out a week, it is guessing.

7. The pre-order bonus cut-off — buy before 20 November

Pre-orders include the Vintage Vice City Pack, and every purchase made before 20 November gets it too. Digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA+ — limited to one redemption per account, and, according to the PlayStation Store terms, it renews automatically at the regular monthly price until you cancel. If you bought after 19 November, check whether the bonus still applies before you assume it does.

What we don't know yet: the size of the day-one update has not been confirmed, no PC release date has been announced, and Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed any pre-order or sales total. Anything claiming those numbers as fact is an estimate being passed off as official — including the 7.71 million figure above, which is a Sensor Tower estimate.

Launch-Week Checklist

  1. Check your platform: PS5 or Xbox Series X|S only. No PC, no PS4, no Xbox One at launch.
  2. Free up to 212GB (about 225GB on Xbox), plus headroom for a day-one update.
  3. Pick your edition: Standard $79.99 or Ultimate $99.99 (digital only).
  4. Boxed copy? It is a download code, not a disc — you still download the full game.
  5. Bonus check: purchases before 20 November get the Vintage Vice City Pack; digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA+.
  6. Ignore the "confirmed sales" posts: the 7.71 million pre-order figure is a Sensor Tower estimate, not a Rockstar total.
