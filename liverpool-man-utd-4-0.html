Liverpool 4–0 Manchester United: Pochettino's Debut Lasted 13 Minutes

By Julia Smith  ·  23 November 2026  ·  Football

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United head coach lasted 13 minutes. Not the whole game — the contest itself. By the 13th minute at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November 2026, United were down to 10 men and 2–0 down, and Liverpool never looked back on their way to a 4–0 win.

The collapse came that fast: Leny Yoro was sent off in the 8th minute, Dominik Szoboszlai curled in the resulting free kick in the 10th, and Florian Wirtz made it 2–0 in the 13th. Liverpool added two more after the break — 18-year-old Rio Ngumoha in the 66th minute and substitute Victor Munoz in the 82nd — while United sank to 19th in the Premier League, one point off the bottom.

How the 13 Minutes Unfolded

  1. 8th minute — red card: Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak as the Liverpool striker raced towards goal, denying a goalscoring opportunity. United were down to 10 men before the game had settled.
  2. 10th minute — 1–0: Dominik Szoboszlai curled home the resulting free kick, low into the bottom corner past Senne Lammens in the United goal.
  3. 13th minute — 2–0: Rio Ngumoha beat Diogo Dalot on the left and crossed for Florian Wirtz, who finished with an acrobatic close-range volley. Two goals, one red card, 13 minutes — Pochettino's debut was over as a contest.
  4. 66th minute — 3–0: Ngumoha swept home a cross from Bradley Barcola to become the youngest player to score against Manchester United in the Premier League, at 18 years old.
  5. 82nd minute — 4–0: Substitute Victor Munoz fired home a ball from Wirtz to complete the rout.

Pochettino responded to the red card by bringing on Harry Maguire in place of Bryan Mbeumo to form a five-man defence. United found a foothold after that, and Lammens made a string of saves — but Liverpool kept coming in waves, and the second-half goals were, by then, inevitable.

United in the Relegation Zone

This was not just a bad day. United sit 19th in the Premier League with just eight points from 11 games, and only one win to show for those 11 matches. They are one point off the bottom of the table. They are in the relegation zone — not relegated, with most of the season still to play — but a club of United's size sitting second from bottom in late November is historic territory. Al Jazeera described it as the worst start for a new Manchester United manager in 100 years.

Liverpool, meanwhile, climbed to fourth on 21 points, seven behind leaders Arsenal. The gap between the two clubs — on the day, and in the table — was the story of the afternoon.

The honest version: United are in the relegation zone, not relegated. There are 27 league games left after this one, and teams have climbed out of 19th before. But eight points from 11 games, one win, and a 4–0 defeat in the new manager's first game is the scale of the job Pochettino has walked into.

5 Things This Match Proves

1. The red card decided it — but it did not excuse it. Yoro's challenge was clumsy, and the sending-off left United chasing the game from the 8th minute. Pochettino criticised the decision to send Yoro off after the game, according to Al Jazeera. Even so, a 10-man side does not have to lose 4–0, and Liverpool created chance after chance all afternoon.

2. Liverpool's young players ran the game. Ngumoha, 18, set up the second goal and scored the third. Wirtz scored one and set up the fourth. This was not a win built on experience alone.

3. The defence is the crisis. United's back line was exposed from the first long ball of the game — Matthijs de Ligt, handed his first Premier League start in 357 days, was caught out by the ball that led to the red card. Al Jazeera noted United had already shipped 30 goals in 15 games this season before this match.

4. There was no new-manager bounce. The one thing a new coach usually gets — a lift in energy and organisation — never had time to appear. The game was gone before the 14th minute.

5. The table does not lie. Fourth against 19th, 21 points against eight. Whatever Pochettino builds next, he starts from the relegation zone, not from mid-table.

What Pochettino Said Next

Pochettino criticised the referee's decision to send off Yoro after the game, according to Al Jazeera's match coverage. Beyond that, the scale of the rebuilding job was the story — Reuters put it plainly: the scale of Pochettino's rebuilding job was laid bare on Sunday.
