GTA 6 Review Bombed: 99/100 From Critics, 5/10 From Players — What Really Happened

By James William  ·  23 November 2026  ·  Viral Drama

Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest entertainment launch in history and one of the best-reviewed games ever made. It is also, on paper, one of the worst-rated games of the year. Critics gave it 99 out of 100. Players gave it about 5 out of 10. Both numbers are real — and neither one tells the whole story.

The game launched on Thursday 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. On Friday, Rockstar Games and its parent company Take-Two Interactive announced $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales — the biggest launch for any entertainment product ever — with 32.5 million copies sold before release. For comparison, GTA V made about $800 million on its first day in 2013. And yet, by the weekend, GTA 6's Metacritic user score sat at roughly 5 out of 10, after falling as low as 3.7 on 20 November.

The 99 vs 5 Gap, Explained

GTA 6 earned a Metacritic score of 99 out of 100 based on 152 professional reviews, matching the record held by the original 1998 release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Its user score, meanwhile, stood at just 5.6 out of 10 on Saturday, according to gaming site GGRecon's count of 34,804 user ratings — many of them flat zeros.

That gap is not an accident. It is the result of three separate things happening at once: fake reviews before launch, a coordinated review-bombing campaign after launch, and a set of genuine complaints from real players. Sorting them out matters, because the headlines keep mixing them up.

Part 1: The Fake Reviews Before Launch

The controversy started before anyone could even play the game. On 16 November, three days before launch, users noticed GTA 6's Metacritic page had already received negative ratings, pulling its user score down to 6.5 out of 10 — from people who could not possibly have played it, let alone finished it. Metacritic removed the fake reviews and temporarily blocked further submissions, as confirmed by Video Games Chronicle on 17 November.

When legitimate user reviews opened on 20 November, the negative ratings came back — and this time they stayed.

Part 2: The Review Bombing

A large share of the low ratings are not genuine reviews at all. According to Forbes, the wave included apparently AI-generated comments — some with their original prompts still visible — as well as users who openly admitted they had not played the game, and newly created accounts whose only contribution was a zero rating for GTA 6.

There is also a fan war behind part of it. Because GTA 6's 99 ties Ocarina of Time's long-standing record, Zelda fans were dragged into the crossfire, and the backlash escalated into open fighting between the two fanbases. None of that has anything to do with the game itself — but it moves the score.

The honest position: there is currently no confirmed evidence of how many of the negative ratings were submitted in bad faith. Some are fake. Some are real players with real complaints. The user score is a mix of both, and anyone quoting it as a straight verdict on the game is misleading you.

Part 3: The Genuine Complaints

Not every low rating can be dismissed. Several recurring complaints come from people who clearly played the game:

  1. The 30fps cap: the game is capped at 30 frames per second on consoles, with no 60fps mode — even on the more powerful PlayStation 5 Pro. Technical analysis by Digital Foundry found the standard PS5 can struggle to maintain 30fps in some situations, while the PS5 Pro delivers more consistent performance.
  2. Too much realism: some players dislike the emphasis on routine activities — shopping, exercising, and maintaining the protagonists' lifestyles — calling the game more of a life simulator than a crime game.
  3. The story focus: the narrative centres on the relationship between the two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the first female co-lead in a mainline 3D GTA game. Some players wanted more traditional criminal progression and less relationship management.
  4. The relationship mechanics: optional couple activities — buying flowers, picking up groceries, gifting jewellery, watching TV together — became a lightning rod online, with some streamers and commentators calling the game a "cuck simulator". Rockstar has said the relationship system and the criminal systems are tracked separately, and players can engage with the relationship or ignore it.

What the Numbers Actually Say

The user score is already climbing back. It fell to 3.7 out of 10 on 20 November, then rose to about 5.1–5.6 by the weekend as more verified buyers rated it — and verified buyers, according to reports, give the game near-perfect marks. The score will keep moving as more players submit ratings, so any single number you see quoted is already out of date.

What is not moving: the sales. $3.79 billion in a day, 32.5 million copies before launch, and a 99 critic score. Whatever the user score says, GTA 6 is the biggest launch entertainment has ever seen — and the review-bombing has not slowed it down.

One thing still has no answer: there is no announced release date for the PC version, and no date for GTA Online in the new game. Analysts expect both in 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed anything.

Quick Answers

Is GTA 6 actually a bad game?
No evidence says so. Critics gave it 99/100, verified buyers rate it near-perfectly, and it sold $3.79 billion in one day. The low user score is a mix of fake reviews, review bombing, and real complaints about the 30fps cap and the story's focus.

Why is the Metacritic user score so low?
Three reasons at once: fake ratings posted before launch (removed by Metacritic), a review-bombing campaign including AI-generated reviews and zero-ratings from accounts that never played it, and genuine criticism of performance and design choices.

Is there a 60fps mode in GTA 6?
No. The game is capped at 30fps on consoles, including PS5 Pro. That is one of the most common real complaints from players.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?
There is no announced date. Analysts expect a PC launch and online mode in 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it.
